MosaicAI on Robinhood Chain

Robinhood's own blockchain is two months old, already the highest fee-generating chain we track, and still mostly speculation. That gap between activity and thesis is exactly where we build.

Robinhood Chain went live on July 1, 20261 — a dedicated Ethereum layer‑2, built on Arbitrum, purpose‑built for round‑the‑clock tokenized stock trading, self‑custody wallets, and “Agentic Accounts” that let an AI model trade on a user's behalf under human‑set limits.2 Two months later, value locked on the chain has grown roughly 175×, and it now generates more daily fee revenue than any other chain we track — including Ethereum mainnet and Coinbase's Base.3

Traction, and the honest caveat

Independent on‑chain analysis puts tokenized‑equity activity at roughly 4% of current volume — the rest is speculative crypto trading, memecoins included.5 That's not a crack in the thesis; it's the timeline. Regulatory clarity is arriving in pieces — the SEC's January 2026 guidance on tokenized securities,6 and Robinhood's own proposal for a federal tokenization framework.7 As that clarity lands, the tokenized‑equity share is what grows.

Where MosaicAI fits

MosaicAI already builds the two things this shift needs at once: audited Solidity for tokenization and custody, and secure, sandboxed AI for the decision layer. Robinhood Chain's Agentic Accounts hand trading authority to an AI model — someone has to build the guardrails, identity, and payment rails those agents run on. Our Agentic Data Room already turns financial disclosures into structured, queryable risk; we're extending it to evaluate issuers, collateral, and DeFi exposure before capital moves on‑chain, not after.
