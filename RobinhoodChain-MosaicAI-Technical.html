01 What Robinhood Chain Is

Robinhood Chain went live on mainnet on July 1, 2026, announced at Robinhood's “The World Is Flat” keynote at the Old Royal Naval College in London, following a public testnet that had already processed more than 200 million transactions.12 It reaches Robinhood's own distribution — roughly 28–30 million funded accounts across 38 countries.14 Technically, it's a dedicated Arbitrum deployment — an “Arbitrum Dedicated Blockchain,” built on the Nitro/Orbit stack — that settles to Ethereum for security and data availability via EIP‑4844 blobs.3 It is fully EVM‑compatible: Solidity and Vyper contracts, standard tooling (Hardhat, Foundry, ethers.js, viem, Wagmi), and JSON‑RPC all work unmodified, with native ERC‑4337 account abstraction for smart‑contract wallets.3

Deployment itself is permissionless — no KYC or allowlist gate exists to put a contract on‑chain: forge create HelloRobinhood --rpc-url $RH_RPC_URL --private-key $PRIVATE_KEY --broadcast, or npx hardhat run scripts/deploy.js --network robinhood, with Blockscout API verification support.3 Bridging in works via LayerZero natively, or through third‑party bridges like Across Protocol and Uniswap's cross‑chain swap. Sequencing is first‑come‑first‑served — there's no priority‑fee auction the way most L1s/L2s work. One centralization detail worth flagging for builders: compliance screening is applied at the sequencer level.19

The chain runs three things: 24/7 trading of tokenized stocks and ETFs, a self‑custody Robinhood Wallet, and — new this quarter — Agentic Trading and an Agentic Credit Card, which let a connected AI agent trade equities (in beta; options, crypto, event contracts, and futures are “coming soon”) or spend from a virtual Gold Card under owner‑set limits.15 Both run on Robinhood's own “Trading” and “Banking” MCP servers — not a third‑party or open agent‑identity standard — with safety rails including isolated sub‑accounts, manual approval, instant disable, and trade preview.15 This supersedes the crypto‑only “Agentic Accounts” framing from launch day: equities, via Robinhood's own proprietary rails, are now the beta focus. Stock tokens are issued as tokenized debt securities by Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited — economic exposure to the underlying equity, not legal share ownership, with corporate actions like dividends and splits handled via an onchain multiplier mechanism19 — a structure carried over from Robinhood's June 2025 “Classic Stock Tokens” launch on Arbitrum One for EU users.4 They're live in 120+ countries through DEXs including Uniswap, Rialto, Lighter, 1inch, and Arcus — not yet in the US, Canada, UK, Switzerland, or UAE.2 Launch partners span Uniswap (a dedicated public AMM), Pleiades (a proprietary‑trading AMM), Chainlink (oracles), Alchemy, BitGo, Morpho (lending), and Lighter (perpetuals).2

These are not interchangeable metrics. growthepie's $6.18M/day is total fees users pay; DefiLlama's $4.01M is revenue the protocol actually retains; CoinDesk's $2.66M is a narrower, dated app‑revenue snapshot from Aug 30. Independent analysis complicates the RWA narrative further: CryptoSlate and CryptoBriefing both estimate tokenized‑equity activity at roughly 4% of on‑chain volume, with GMGN and Pons — two independent, permissionless meme‑token platforms built on top of the chain, not Robinhood products18 — together with Uniswap driving roughly 88–90% of daily revenue — echoing Base's own early speculation problem, scam tokens included.816 For a business built on the tokenization thesis, that's a signal to track, not a reason to dismiss the chain: stock‑token trading itself is real and growing — Uniswap alone processed $1.5B in stock‑token trades in the chain's first six weeks, up from a reported ~$13M in the earliest days20 — and CEO Vlad Tenev cites on‑chain equity trading volume reaching $9B in 2026, up 800%+ year‑to‑date, across Robinhood's tokenized‑equity products.17 Fee revenue, TVL, and active addresses are real and growing regardless of what's driving them today.

03 Competitive Landscape

Robinhood isn't alone in tokenizing equities on‑chain. Coinbase is building toward 1:1 asset‑backed tokenized stocks on Base, where tokenization is one use case among many rather than the chain's reason for existing.9 Kraken acquired Backed Finance — issuer of the xStocks tokenized‑equity product — in December 2025, favoring multi‑chain distribution over a dedicated L2.9 Robinhood's bet is structurally different: a chain purpose‑built around its own brokerage distribution and regulatory relationships, rather than tokenization layered onto a general‑purpose chain — and the distribution numbers in the table below back that up.14

04 Architecture & Where MosaicAI Plugs In

Ethereum anchors settlement and data availability. Arbitrum's Nitro/Orbit stack sequences transactions and posts fraud proofs for the dedicated chain. Robinhood Chain itself hosts the application layer — Stock Tokens, the self‑custody Wallet built on ERC‑4337 smart accounts, DeFi primitives (Uniswap and Pleiades AMMs, Morpho lending paying roughly 7% APY on USDG, Lighter perpetuals), and Chainlink oracles for price feeds.2 MosaicAI's service layer sits directly on top of — and wires into — specific components in that stack.

07 Barriers to Entry

Two skill sets rarely live in the same team. Blockchain shops rarely carry AI‑safety expertise; AI shops rarely ship audited Solidity or reason well about securities‑adjacent compliance. MosaicAI already operates at the GCP/Vertex/Gemini/Claude level for secure, sandboxed AI, and at the Ethereum/Solidity/DeFi level for smart contracts — the combination is the moat, not either half alone.

Second, Agentic Data Room is a shipped product, not a roadmap slide; extending it into RWA diligence is incremental engineering against a working pipeline, not a cold start. Third, Robinhood just confirmed it's building its own proprietary agent rails (Trading/Banking MCP servers) rather than adopting open standards — which means our bet on open, interoperable agent infrastructure (ERC‑8004, x402) isn't competing with Robinhood's roadmap, it's serving everyone Robinhood's consumer‑app rails don't reach: third‑party funds, issuers, and institutional agents.

08 Risks & Open Questions

Speculation, not tokenization, drives current volume

~4% of on‑chain activity is tokenized equities; GMGN, Pons, and Uniswap together drive ~88–90% of daily revenue, echoing Base's early days.816 The tokenization thesis is a bet on trajectory, not a description of today's chain — though stock‑token volume itself is growing quickly underneath it.

Metrics are easy to misquote

Fees (growthepie), revenue (DefiLlama), and app‑revenue (CoinDesk) are different metrics from different snapshots and dates. The “beats Ethereum” headline is a daily spike; on a trailing 30‑day basis Robinhood Chain reportedly ranks closer to 5th.16

Regulatory framework is incomplete

The SEC's January 2026 guidance applies existing securities law to tokenized instruments and flags bankruptcy/issuer risk for synthetic tokens.10 Robinhood's proposed federal tokenization framework and a reported 24/7‑trading “innovation exemption” are not finalized.11

Robinhood chose proprietary agent rails over open standards

Agentic Trading and the Agentic Credit Card run on Robinhood's own Trading/Banking MCP servers, not ERC‑8004 or x402.15 That's now confirmed, not speculative — our agent‑infrastructure thesis bets on outside‑builder demand for an interoperable alternative, not on Robinhood adopting open standards itself.

Issuers don't always welcome tokenization

AMC's CEO publicly objected to an AMC‑linked tokenized stock and ordered a legal review12 — a reminder with real implications for anyone building issuer‑facing diligence tooling.

Base‑layer direction is still moving

Ethereum's “Lean Ethereum” roadmap (July 2026) points toward a multi‑year overhaul favoring a RISC‑V‑style execution path, while Arbitrum's team has favored WASM/Stylus.13 Worth watching; no direct bearing on Robinhood Chain today.

Build for where the chain is headed, not just where it is

Robinhood Chain is two months old, briefly outearned Ethereum on daily revenue, and just opened itself to AI agents — on its own proprietary terms. The gap between today's speculative volume and the tokenization thesis, and between Robinhood's closed agent rails and an open one, is a build window for issuer diligence, agent guardrails, audited contracts, and the analytics layer underneath all of it.
