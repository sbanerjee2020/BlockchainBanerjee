01 — Executive summary

No single chain wins every dimension

Ethereum still holds the deepest liquidity and strongest security budget. Two purpose-built institutional networks — Canton and Provenance — already move more real-world value than their DeFi dashboards suggest. Three 2025-launched fintech chains (Circle's Arc, Stripe's Tempo, Robinhood Chain) are fast and cheap on paper, but only Tempo and Robinhood Chain are actually live, and both are under a year old.

04 — Finality & throughput

"Instant" usually means two different things

Every Ethereum L2 (Base, Ink, Robinhood Chain) can confirm a transaction in under two seconds — but that's a sequencer promise, not irreversible settlement. True finality still depends on Ethereum's own ~12–20 minute window, or in Ink's case, a multi-day fraud-proof challenge period before funds can safely leave the chain.

06 — Sample calculations

Turning the numbers above into dollars

Grounded in a real candidate asset: the FT Vest Gold Strategy Target Income ETF (Cboe BZX: IGLD), a First Trust/Vest Financial fund holding U.S. Treasuries and FLEX Options referencing the SPDR® Gold Trust. As of August 25, 2026 it held $611,989,041 in net assets across 26,850,002 shares outstanding, NAV $22.79/share, expense ratio 0.85%. First Trust — IGLD fund page

A. The hidden cost of "soft" finality on an IGLD creation-unit redemption

ETF shares move in and out of existence in fixed-size blocks called creation units, handled by Authorized Participants (APs) — a standard block size is 50,000 shares. At IGLD's August 25, 2026 NAV, one creation unit is worth 50,000 × $22.79 = $1,139,500. Until that redemption reaches true settlement finality, the AP's capital is functionally locked up. At a 5% annual opportunity-cost rate, every second of waiting costs money.

opportunity cost = $1,139,500 × 5% ÷ 31,536,000 sec = $0.001807 / second

At creation-unit scale, most chains are effectively free — the number that matters is the last row of any redemption path that actually needs irreversible settlement. An AP redeeming IGLD through Ink would carry roughly $546 of opportunity cost per creation unit, purely from the 3.5-day exit window. Scaled to a full-fund wind-down of IGLD's entire $611.99M AUM, the same 3.5-day Ink exit window costs approximately $293,556. Illustrative 5% rate; actual AP financing costs vary.

One token per share is the most granular — and least realistic — design: real issuers mint total supply in a handful of transactions and only transact at the creation-unit or secondary-trade level, so treat this as a worst-case ceiling, not an operating cost. Even so, the spread is instructive: minting every IGLD share individually on Canton's institutional bandwidth pricing would cost more than five years of the fund's entire expense-ratio revenue ($611.99M × 0.85% ≈ $5.2M/year). On Base, Ethereum, or Robinhood Chain, the same exercise costs under 9 basis points of AUM.

The full picture, chain by chain

Ethereum Mainnet

Mature

The settlement layer everything else is judged against.

ArchitectureL1, Proof-of-Stake since 2022. The 2025–26 Pectra and Fusaka upgrades raised the block gas limit from 45M to 150M and expanded blob capacity for L2 data — Ethereum now functions primarily as the settlement/data-availability layer beneath a large rollup ecosystem. Ethereum Foundation
Dev ecosystem1M+ lifetime developers, the largest of any chain by a wide margin. ERC-3643 (compliance-ready security tokens) and ERC-7540 (async vaults for real-world settlement cycles) are the RWA-relevant standards. thirdweb
Security~902,557 active validators, ~$70–80B in staked value. No successful consensus-layer attack since the 2022 Merge. beaconcha.in
RWA tractionBlackRock BUIDL (~$2.78B), Franklin Templeton BENJI (~$828M), Ondo USDY (~$1.4B), Circle USYC (~$2.88B). DefiLlama

Coinbase Base

Established

Cheap, fast, and one company controls the sequencer.

ArchitectureOptimistic rollup on the OP Stack, reached "Stage 1" decentralization in April 2025 with permissionless fault proofs. Coinbase alone still runs the sequencer, and Base is migrating off the shared OP Stack onto its own Reth-based stack in 2026. Base Blog
Dev ecosystemFull EVM tooling parity with Ethereum. Coinbase's Project Diamond and Coinbase Prime ($350B+ institutional AUM) anchor institutional access. CoinDesk
SecuritySequencer halted block production twice in 24 hours in June 2026. Upgrade authority sits with a 9-of-12 multisig, not a fully trustless system yet. Our Crypto Talk
RWA tractionLargest L2 by stablecoin liquidity ($4.74B), but RWA issuance itself is thin — Centrifuge's Morpho market (Anemoy, Midas mTBILL, Hashnote USYC) is the concrete example. Centrifuge

Canton Network

Established

Built by Digital Asset specifically so a bank never has to show its counterparties' data to a stranger.

ArchitectureA permissioned "network of networks" using Daml. Sub-transaction privacy means each validator only ever sees the data relevant to its own parties — a fundamentally different model from public EVM chains. Canton
Dev ecosystemDaml has a steeper learning curve than Solidity but stronger compile-time guarantees. 600+ institutions participate, including Goldman Sachs, BNY Mellon, Deutsche Börse, and DTCC as Foundation co-chair. BlockEden
Live productsJPM Coin deployed on Canton (Jan 2026); DTCC's Treasury-tokenization MVP targets H1 2026 production; Goldman Sachs + BNY Mellon's tokenized money-market fund launched July 2025. Coinspeaker

10 — Decision framework

Which chain, for which asset

For a broad, multi-asset platform, the honest answer is a portfolio of chains, not one winner. Here's how to split by asset type.

Asset typeBest fit todayWhy
Cash equivalents, tokenized treasuries, money-market fundsEthereumDeepest liquidity — BUIDL, BENJI, USYC all live here first
Private credit, real estate, structured lendingProvenanceProven at $20B+ scale with protocol-level KYC/AML
Interbank settlement, bank-to-bank deposits, repoCantonJPM Coin, Broadridge DLR, DTCC — all live, all regulated
Public equities / ETFsEthereum / BaseOndo Global Markets and Securitize infrastructure are mature; Robinhood Chain's custody model needs its EU inquiry resolved first
Consumer-facing distributionBaseCoinbase Prime's reach ($350B+ AUM) plus sub-cent fees
Cross-border, stablecoin-native paymentsWatch Arc / TempoPurpose-built for this, but both need 6–12 more months of live data
Bottom line for a 2026 build: anchor primary rails on Ethereum for liquidity and Base for cost/reach, add Provenance or Canton for the private-credit and institutional-settlement legs, and treat Circle's Arc, Stripe's Tempo, and Robinhood Chain as re-evaluation candidates in 6–12 months rather than launch infrastructure.
