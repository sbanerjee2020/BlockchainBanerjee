Tokenized Stocks: Two Roads

OKXICE, the ICE + OKX venue for tokenized shares, launched Nov 23 even though 56 of the 62 companies it first targeted objected. The open question is which model issuers will accept: a third-party venue that lists unless you opt out, or the issuer-led path Nasdaq and Kraken parent Payward plan to test in Q2 2027.

The objection count

56 of 62 issuers said no to OKXICE

Objected (56)Did not object (6)

Per public notices Oct 4 – Nov 5. More companies, including some not on the first list, objected by Nov 20. Launch names: Coinbase, Robinhood, Strategy, Circle, Securitize, BitGo. Fidelity, Direxion, eToro, Strive and Vida Global have since joined.

Why they objected

Unready, not opposed

  • The window was too short. 30 days landed on earnings season and the midterms. Issuers asked for time to learn rather than rejecting the idea (Seth Ginns, Franklin Templeton).
  • Investor relations pushed back. Executives were interested, but IR teams worried about timing announcements when the stock trades 24/7 (Ran Grushkowsky, MassPay).
  • Internal gaps. Some heads of digital assets did not know who in their company had opted out (Carlos Domingo, Securitize).
  • Unfamiliar territory. Most companies had not yet studied tokenization; OKXICE co-chair Andrew Cuomo says they warm to it as they learn about 24/7 global access.

Why tokenization

What institutions and investors get

24/7 trading

Round-the-clock, global markets instead of exchange hours.

Faster settlement

Ownership moves near-instantly versus multi-day settlement through intermediaries.

Fractional ownership

Smaller slices of high-priced assets for more investors.

Collateral mobility

Securities become easier to pledge and move as collateral.

Global distribution

Tenev: a way to deliver access to U.S. stocks worldwide.

Bigger market

Clear Street's Owen Lau: it grows the addressable market, though revenue is a long way off.

For exchanges, it is also defensive: a way to keep control of trading infrastructure as crypto-native venues bring traditional assets onchain.

Squawk Box · Sep 9, 20263:05

Vlad Tenev, Robinhood CEO

  • Tokenization lets Robinhood distribute access to U.S. stocks globally.
  • Responding to AMC's Adam Aron: issuers control their stock's rights, not every product that references it.
  • Stock tokens are issued by a separate entity, backed by real shares, structured as debt.
  • Holders get no votes; Robinhood has not said how it will vote the underlying shares.

Carlos Domingo, Securitize CEO
