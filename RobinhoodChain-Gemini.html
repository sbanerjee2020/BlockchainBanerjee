4. Mathematical Risk Model: Dynamic Liquidation Thresholds

To eliminate liquidation contagion while maximizing capital efficiency for tokenized stocks, Mosaic AI deploys dynamic liquidation threshold (Lt) calculations refreshed every 100ms:

Dynamic Liquidation Threshold Formulation

Lt = L0 - (α · σ24h) - (β · Slippagedepth) + (γ · ADR_Score)

Where:

• L0: Baseline liquidation threshold (e.g., 0.85 for S&P 500 equities).

• σ24h: 24-hour annualized historical and implied volatility.

• Slippagedepth: Dynamic market slippage modeled across Arcus and Uniswap pools for a $500,000 liquidation block.

• ADR_Score: Real-time off-chain solvency confidence score [0.0 to 1.0].

• α, β, γ: Sensitivity calibration coefficients (α = 0.15, β = 0.20, γ = 0.05).

Worked Calculation ($NVDA Stock Token Example)

Parameters: L0 = 0.85 | σ24h = 0.32 | Slippage = 0.02 | ADR_Score = 0.98

Lt = 0.85 - (0.15 × 0.32) - (0.20 × 0.02) + (0.05 × 0.98)

Lt = 0.85 - 0.048 - 0.004 + 0.049 = 0.847 (84.7%)
